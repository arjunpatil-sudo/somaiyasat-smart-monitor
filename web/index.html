1 · Live health monitor Exp 2–3 · DNN

Set a telemetry reading, or pick a scenario. The deep neural network from Exp 3 (128-64-32 with BatchNorm) classifies the satellite's state.

-

The class probabilities come from softmax. The inputs are standardised with the training-set mean and standard deviation.

2 · Orbit replay with LSTM forecast Exp 7–8 · LSTM

Telemetry from a test run the models never saw, which has a battery-ageing fault. At every minute the LSTM reads the past 60 minutes and forecasts the next 15 minutes of battery voltage and temperature. The DNN labels the current state.
